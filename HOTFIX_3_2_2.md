# DIRTLINE MX Arcade 3.2.2

Runtime regression fix based on Android gameplay capture.

- repairs touchscreen control construction and anchors controls to the screen bottom
- adds a race-start watchdog so the grid cannot remain inactive after GO
- makes player engine idle audible on the grid and adds an AI engine ambience bed
- moves the START arch behind the riders and reduces intrusive trackside billboards
- expands CI smoke testing to require visible controls, active racers, loaded engine audio, player forward motion, and AI forward motion
